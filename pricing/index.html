The problem

Every missed call is a job for someone else

You’re busy on the job. The phone rings, it goes to voicemail, and the customer doesn’t leave a message. They call the next business on Google, and that business gets the job.

It adds up fast. Miss 8 calls a month at $400 a job and $3,200 goes to a competitor.
