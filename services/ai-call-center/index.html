The short version

Your contact center, with a tireless first line

Most contact centers spend a large share of agent time on calls that follow a script: where is my order, can I reschedule, what are your hours, has my payment gone through. Those calls are predictable, which makes them ideal for an AI agent connected to the right systems.

We deploy AI voice agents as the first line or as overflow: they answer instantly, authenticate callers, complete routine tasks through your APIs and hand anything complex to a human with the context already captured. Your team handles fewer, better calls, and peak-hour queues stop costing you customers.

How it works

From call drivers to live queues

We start with the calls that are high volume and low complexity, then expand.

  1. 01

    Analyze call drivers

    We review recordings or reason codes to find the most automatable call types.

  2. 02

    Design and integrate

    Conversation flows, verification and API integrations for those call types.

  3. 03

    Shadow and pilot

    The AI takes a small share of one queue while supervisors review every call.

  4. 04

    Scale by queue

    Expand queue by queue, tuning from transcripts and containment data.
