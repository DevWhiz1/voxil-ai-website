The short version

Why minutes matter

Research on lead response has found the odds of reaching and qualifying a lead fall steeply after the first few minutes. Leads from ads and quote forms often contact several businesses at once, and the first one to respond sets the terms.

Speed-to-lead automation removes the delay. Every new lead gets an immediate, personalized response and a call within a minute, from an AI agent or a routed team member, and every response time is measured so you can prove it’s working.
