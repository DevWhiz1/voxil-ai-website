Quick answer

An AI follow-up system automatically re-engages leads who haven’t responded, new inquiries, open quotes, missed appointments and old contacts. It sends personalized messages across SMS, email, WhatsApp and voice on a schedule, understands replies with AI, answers questions and books appointments, stopping as soon as the person responds or opts out.

The short version

The fortune is in the follow-up, if anyone actually does it

Every business knows follow-up matters. Few do it consistently, because it depends on a busy person remembering to send the fourth message. An AI follow-up system removes the memory problem entirely.

Unlike old drip campaigns, the AI reads replies and responds intelligently, answering a question, handling a "not now", or booking a time, so a follow-up becomes a conversation, not just another automated email.
