The short version

Stop guessing what happens on the phone

Most managers listen to a handful of calls a week, if that. Patterns in lost deals, rude answers, missed upsells or compliance slips go unnoticed until they show up in revenue or complaints.

AI call analytics reviews every call against the checklist you care about: did the agent ask for the appointment, quote correctly, confirm details, follow the required disclosures. It flags the calls worth listening to and surfaces trends across the whole team.
