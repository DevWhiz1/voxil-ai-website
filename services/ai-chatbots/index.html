Quick answer

An AI chatbot is a conversational assistant powered by a large language model and trained on your own content, website, pricing, policies and documents. Unlike rule-based bots, it understands free-text questions, answers accurately from your approved knowledge, captures lead details and completes actions like booking or order lookups.

The short version

Not the chatbot your visitors learned to ignore

Old decision-tree bots failed because they could only follow scripts. Modern LLM chatbots understand what the visitor actually typed, answer from your real documentation, and ask the follow-up questions a good salesperson would.

We build chatbots as part of your funnel: they capture name, email and phone at the right moment, push qualified leads to your CRM, book calls into your calendar, and alert your team in Slack or SMS when a hot conversation needs a human.
