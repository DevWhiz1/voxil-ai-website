Quick answer

GoHighLevel support is an ongoing service that maintains and improves a GHL account: fixing broken workflows, resolving messaging and deliverability issues, making funnel and website edits, building new automations, updating AI settings and monitoring account health, usually on a monthly retainer.

Because GHL changes every week, and so does your business

GoHighLevel ships updates constantly, carriers change messaging rules, and your offers and team evolve. Without someone watching, small issues become lost leads.
