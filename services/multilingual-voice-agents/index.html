The short version

Language shouldn’t decide who gets served

When a caller can’t communicate comfortably, they hang up, misunderstand instructions or never call back. Hiring bilingual staff for every shift and every language is rarely realistic for small and mid-sized businesses.

A multilingual voice agent answers in the caller’s language from the first sentence, handles code-switching between languages, and keeps one set of answers, prices and policies, so information is consistent whatever language the conversation happens in.
