Quick answer

GoHighLevel AI refers to the AI features inside the GoHighLevel CRM, including Conversation AI for SMS, chat and social replies, Voice AI for answering calls, and AI steps inside workflows. Configured well, they respond to leads instantly, book appointments into GHL calendars and move contacts through your pipeline automatically.

The short version

The AI is included. The configuration isn’t.

Most GoHighLevel accounts have AI features switched off, or switched on with a generic prompt that gives vague answers and never books. The difference between "we tried the AI" and "the AI books appointments" is training, guardrails and workflow design.

We configure GHL’s native AI around your offer and calendars, build the workflows that react to what it learns, and, where you need more control, better voices or complex logic, connect external agents built on Vapi or Retell AI to your GHL account.

  • Voice AI agent configuration
  • AI-powered workflow automation
