Quick answer

A knowledge base AI chatbot uses retrieval-augmented generation (RAG): it searches your approved documents for the most relevant passages and uses them to write an answer, with citations. It keeps answers grounded in your content, can respect access permissions, and is used for customer support and internal knowledge.

The short version

Why a generic chatbot isn’t enough

General AI models don’t know your prices, policies or procedures, and when they don’t know, they may guess. For customer support or internal questions, a confident wrong answer is worse than no answer.

RAG solves this by retrieving the relevant parts of your own content before answering. The chatbot answers only from those sources, shows where the answer came from, and hands off or says it doesn’t know when the documents don’t cover the question.

What's included

  • Retrieval tuning and answer formatting with citations
  • Access controls for internal or customer-specific content
