Quick answer

A conversational AI IVR replaces touch-tone phone menus with natural speech. Instead of pressing numbers, callers explain why they’re calling; the AI identifies the intent, resolves simple requests such as hours or appointment changes, and routes other calls to the correct team with context attached.

The short version

Why phone menus frustrate callers

Traditional IVRs make callers guess which option fits, listen to long menus and often end up in the wrong department. Many simply press zero or hang up. Every misroute costs agent time and caller patience.

A conversational IVR asks one question, “How can I help today?”, and understands the answer. It can confirm details, answer simple questions immediately and send the caller to the right team, with the reason for the call already noted.
