The short version

Voicemail is where leads go to die

Research on small-business phone traffic has repeatedly found that a large share of calls go unanswered, and most callers who reach voicemail don’t leave a message. They call a competitor instead. See our missed-call statistics for the data.

AI phone answering fixes the gap without changing how you work. Forward your line when you’re busy, closed or on a job; the AI handles the conversation and your phone lights up with a summary you can act on, or a booked appointment you don’t need to touch.
