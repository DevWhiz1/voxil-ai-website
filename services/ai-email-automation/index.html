The short version

Email is still where a lot of business happens

Quote requests, booking changes, supplier messages and support questions pile up in shared inboxes. Important emails get buried, response times slip and details are retyped into other systems.

An AI inbox assistant sorts every email by type and urgency, routes it to the right person, drafts a reply that uses your policies and the customer’s history, and logs the important details. Your team reviews and sends, and simple requests can be answered automatically.

  • Reply drafting from your knowledge base and CRM
