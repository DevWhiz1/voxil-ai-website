Quick answer

Workflow automation is the use of software to run business processes automatically, moving data between apps, triggering tasks, sending notifications and making rule-based or AI-assisted decisions. It reduces manual work and errors in areas like lead handling, onboarding, invoicing, reporting and support.

The short version

If a person copies it, checks it or chases it, automate it

Sales reps typically spend only a fraction of their week actually selling; the rest disappears into admin. Operations and finance teams face the same drag. Workflow automation reclaims that time.

With AI, automation now reaches tasks that used to need a human: reading emails and PDFs, classifying requests, extracting data from documents and drafting responses. We combine rules and AI so workflows are fast and dependable.
