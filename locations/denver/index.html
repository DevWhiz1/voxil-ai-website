Colorado’s AI and privacy rules

Colorado has passed one of the first comprehensive state AI laws, aimed at "high-risk" AI used in consequential decisions, alongside the Colorado Privacy Act. Customer-service and booking agents are typically lower-risk, but we document how systems make decisions and keep humans in the loop. Outbound AI calls and texts to US numbers fall under the TCPA and FCC rules, the FCC confirmed in 2024 that AI-generated voices count as "artificial" voices, so automated outbound calls need prior express consent.

General information, not legal advice, confirm specifics with your counsel.
