Different recording rules across DC, Maryland and Virginia

DC and Virginia allow one-party consent to record calls, while Maryland requires all parties to consent. Because callers come from all three, our agents disclose recording on every call. We also scope data handling carefully for organizations with contractual security requirements. For outbound work, the TCPA applies to US numbers, and the FCC treats AI-generated voices as “artificial” voices, so automated calls need prior express consent.
