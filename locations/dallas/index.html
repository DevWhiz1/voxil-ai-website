Texas privacy and calling rules

Texas’ Data Privacy and Security Act (in effect since 2024) adds consumer-data obligations, and Texas has its own telemarketing registration rules. Outbound AI calls and texts to US numbers fall under the TCPA and FCC rules, the FCC confirmed in 2024 that AI-generated voices count as "artificial" voices, so automated outbound calls need prior express consent. We build consent, opt-out and data-minimization into every build.
