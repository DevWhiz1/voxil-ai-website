PIPEDA, Ontario’s PHIPA and CASL

Ontario clinics and other health information custodians are covered by PHIPA, Ontario’s health privacy law, alongside federal PIPEDA for commercial activity. We keep health details out of messages, build CASL-compliant consent and unsubscribe handling into every email and SMS flow, and respect CRTC telemarketing rules and the National Do Not Call List.
