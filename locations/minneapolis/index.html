Minnesota privacy and recording rules

The Minnesota Consumer Data Privacy Act took effect in 2025, giving residents rights over their personal data and requiring clear privacy practices. Minnesota allows one-party consent for recording, but our agents disclose recording on every call as best practice. For outbound work, the TCPA applies to US numbers, and the FCC treats AI-generated voices as “artificial” voices, so automated calls need prior express consent.
