South Australian recording law and national privacy rules

South Australia’s Surveillance Devices Act regulates recording private conversations, so our agents disclose recording up front and record only with consent. The federal Privacy Act 1988, the Spam Act 2003 and the Do Not Call Register apply across Australia.
