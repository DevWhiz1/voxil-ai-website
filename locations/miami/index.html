Florida calling and recording rules

Florida requires all-party consent to record calls and has its own telemarketing law (the Florida Telephone Solicitation Act) on top of federal rules. Outbound AI calls and texts to US numbers fall under the TCPA and FCC rules, the FCC confirmed in 2024 that AI-generated voices count as "artificial" voices, so automated outbound calls need prior express consent. We configure disclosures, consent and calling windows for Florida.
