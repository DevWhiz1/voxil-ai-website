Data handling and local payments

Pakistan’s dedicated data-protection law is still evolving, with the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA) 2016 currently relevant. We follow international best practice for consent and data minimisation, and can integrate local payment options such as JazzCash and Easypaisa where supported.
