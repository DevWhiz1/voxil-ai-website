GDPR, CNIL guidance and Bloctel

Personal data is processed under GDPR, with the CNIL as the French supervisory authority. Telemarketing calls to consumers must respect the Bloctel opposition list, and France has been tightening consent rules for commercial calls. The EU AI Act adds transparency duties for chatbots and voice agents, so ours disclose that they are AI.
