The the UK market

WhatsApp-first customers and strict marketing rules

UK customers use WhatsApp heavily for everyday communication, and increasingly expect businesses to be reachable there. Pairing WhatsApp automation with an AI phone agent covers the two channels most inquiries arrive on.

UK rules on marketing calls and texts (PECR) and data protection (UK GDPR) are stricter than in the US. We design consent, lawful basis and data retention in from the start, and use British English voices and spellings so the experience feels local.
