Texas rules, bilingual disclosures

We deliver consent and recording disclosures in both English and Spanish, and follow Texas privacy law. Outbound AI calls and texts to US numbers fall under the TCPA and FCC rules, the FCC confirmed in 2024 that AI-generated voices count as "artificial" voices, so automated outbound calls need prior express consent.
