California bot disclosure, recording and privacy

California requires bots to disclose they are bots when used to incentivise a sale in online communications, requires all-party consent to record calls, and grants consumers rights under the CCPA/CPRA. We configure clear AI disclosure, recording notices and data-handling documentation for every California deployment.
