GDPR, opt-in telemarketing and the EU AI Act

We design for the GDPR and the Dutch data protection authority’s guidance. Since 2021 the Netherlands requires opt-in consent for telemarketing calls to consumers, so outbound AI calls only go to people who agreed to be contacted, and every agent discloses that it is AI as the EU AI Act requires.
