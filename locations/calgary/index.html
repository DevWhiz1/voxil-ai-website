Alberta PIPA and CASL

Private-sector organizations in Alberta are covered by the province’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), and CASL governs commercial texts and emails across Canada. Telemarketing follows CRTC rules and the National Do Not Call List. We build consent records and unsubscribe handling into every flow.
