The Canada market

Bilingual service and some of the world’s strictest anti-spam rules

Canadian businesses often need to serve customers in English and French, and across six time zones. AI agents make bilingual, around-the-clock coverage realistic for small and mid-sized teams.

Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) is stricter than US email rules, consent requirements are explicit and penalties can be significant, so follow-up and marketing automation must be built carefully. We design consent capture and records into every system.
