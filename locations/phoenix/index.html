Compliant calling, texting and recording

Arizona observes Mountain Standard Time year-round, so we schedule calling windows carefully. Outbound AI calls and texts to US numbers fall under the TCPA and FCC rules, the FCC confirmed in 2024 that AI-generated voices count as "artificial" voices, so automated outbound calls need prior express consent. SMS requires A2P 10DLC registration.
