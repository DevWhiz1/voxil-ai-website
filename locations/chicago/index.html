Illinois BIPA and recording consent

Illinois’ Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA) covers voiceprints, so voice systems must not create speaker-identification profiles without informed written consent. Illinois also restricts recording private conversations without all parties’ consent. We design voice agents that avoid voiceprint biometrics and disclose recording clearly.

General information, not legal advice, confirm specifics with your counsel.
