Texas privacy, healthcare and calling rules

We build HIPAA-aware systems for Houston healthcare providers and follow Texas’ Data Privacy and Security Act. Outbound AI calls and texts to US numbers fall under the TCPA and FCC rules, the FCC confirmed in 2024 that AI-generated voices count as "artificial" voices, so automated outbound calls need prior express consent.
