GDPR and the EU AI Act

We document lawful basis, processors, retention and cross-border transfers under the GDPR, and follow the EU AI Act’s transparency rules, which apply from August 2026 and require that people are told when they are interacting with an AI system. Marketing calls and messages also follow each country’s ePrivacy rules.
