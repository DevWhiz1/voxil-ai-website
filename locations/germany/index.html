GDPR, German consent rules and the EU AI Act

We design for the GDPR and Germany’s Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG). German law requires prior consent for advertising calls to consumers and makes recording a conversation without consent a criminal offense, so our agents only call consented contacts and ask before recording. Every agent discloses that it is AI, as the EU AI Act requires.
