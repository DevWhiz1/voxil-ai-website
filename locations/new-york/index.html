New York compliance, designed in

Outbound AI calls and texts to US numbers fall under the TCPA and FCC rules, the FCC confirmed in 2024 that AI-generated voices count as "artificial" voices, so automated outbound calls need prior express consent. New York’s SHIELD Act also sets data-security expectations for businesses holding New Yorkers’ private information, and NYC regulates automated tools used in hiring decisions. We scope data retention, recording disclosures and consent handling up front.
