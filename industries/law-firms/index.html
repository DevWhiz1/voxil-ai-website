Real talk

What owners ask us before they start

Honest answers to the pushback we hear most.

“Clients need a human for sensitive matters.”

Yes, and they get one. The AI handles the first contact and logistics with care, then hands to your team with the details already captured.

“What about confidentiality?”

We minimize data collected in chat and SMS, restrict access and configure retention to your policies. Advice and case discussion stay with attorneys.

“We already use an answering service.”

An AI intake agent can screen, book and update your case management system in real time, rather than just taking messages.
