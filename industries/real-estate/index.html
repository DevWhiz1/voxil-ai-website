The problem

Where real-estate teams lose deals

Leads cost more every year. Most are lost to speed and follow-up, not price.

Slow first response

Portal leads contact several agents; the first to respond usually gets the conversation.

Long buying cycles

Many leads aren’t ready for months, and nobody follows up long enough.

Showing coordination

Back-and-forth scheduling eats agent time and loses momentum.

After-hours enquiries

Evenings and weekends are when buyers browse, and when offices are closed.
