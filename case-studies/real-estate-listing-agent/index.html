The challenge

Night and weekend inquiries went cold before an agent could reply, and agents spent evenings answering the same listing questions.

What we built

  • A property Q&A agent that answers listing questions at any hour
  • Showing requests captured and booked into agents’ calendars
  • Every conversation logged in the CRM

The result

More inquiries converted to booked showings with less manual follow-up.

How we delivered it

From first call to live system

The real estate project followed the same four stages as every Voxil AI build, so the team could see progress every week and nothing went live untested.

  1. 01

    Scope the problem

    A short call to map where inquiries were being lost, which systems were involved and what a good outcome looked like.

  2. 02

    Build & integrate

    The agent was trained on approved business information and connected to the existing calendar, CRM and messaging tools.

  3. 03

    Supervised pilot

    It ran on a slice of real traffic first, with transcripts reviewed so answers, tone and hand-offs could be tuned.

  4. 04

    Launch & improve

    Full rollout with documentation for the team, followed by tuning based on real conversations.

Could the same approach work for your business? If your team answers the same questions every day, misses calls or leads after hours, or re-types data between tools, the answer is usually yes. We’ll tell you honestly on a free 30-minute call, including when a simpler fix would do.
