The challenge

Support volume scaled with ad spend, but support headcount could not keep up, and customers waited too long for simple answers.

What we built

  • A branded chatbot connected to live order data
  • Answers for shipping, returns and order-status questions
  • A clear hand-off path to a human agent with the conversation attached

The result

A large share of tickets were resolved automatically while customer satisfaction stayed stable.

How we delivered it

From first call to live system

The ecommerce project followed the same four stages as every Voxil AI build, so the team could see progress every week and nothing went live untested.

  1. 01

    Sort the ticket backlog

    We grouped recent support tickets by topic and found that shipping, returns and order-status questions made up most of the volume.

  2. 02

    Connect live order data

    The chatbot was connected to the order system so it could look up real orders, instead of reciting help-center articles.

  3. 03

    Launch on one channel

    It went live on the website chat first, with every conversation logged and a one-click hand-off to an agent with the history attached.

  4. 04

    Expand and refine

    Answers and hand-off rules were refined from real transcripts before the bot was added to more entry points.

If your support volume rises with every ad campaign and most tickets are “where is my order?” or “how do I return this?”, a chatbot connected to live order data usually pays for itself quickly.
