In detail

The four models, in detail

Same team behind all four. What changes is who holds the brief and how you pay for it.

01

Done-for-you fulfillment

You close the deal and send the scope. We build the whole system inside your client’s sub-account: pipelines, workflows, funnels, calendars, integrations and the automations behind them. You get a Loom walkthrough and written notes so your account manager can present it as your team’s work. You keep the margin and the relationship.

Best when: you sell a defined package again and again, and delivery is what limits how many you can sell.

Full detail
02

Dedicated GHL assistant

A GoHighLevel-trained assistant works only on your accounts, inside your Slack and task board, during the hours you choose. Routine builds, edits, imports and reporting get done daily, and anything complex is reviewed by a senior engineer before it ships.

Best when: you have a steady stream of GHL tasks every week and want a consistent person who learns your clients.

Full detail
03

White label support desk

“The calendar stopped syncing.” “The automation fired twice.” “Why didn’t this lead get the text?” We take those tickets against your accounts, find the real cause instead of restarting the workflow and hoping, and report back in your channel, in your name.

Best when: you have a retained client base and support volume, not project volume, is what’s burning your team.

Full detail
04

Per-project overflow

One build, one fixed price, no retainer and no commitment past delivery. It’s how most partnerships start, because you judge us on real work instead of a sales call. It’s also the right shape for work outside your usual stack: a WordPress site, a Shopify store, a CRM migration or an AI voice agent.

Best when: demand is lumpy, or you sold something your team doesn’t normally build.

Fit check

Who this fits, and who it doesn’t

A partnership that isn’t a fit wastes both our time. This is the honest version.

A good fit if…

  • You sell GoHighLevel, AI or web services and delivery is the bottleneck
  • You’re turning down projects, or quietly missing deadlines on the ones you took
  • You want capacity without payroll, recruiting or ramp-up time
  • Clients are asking for AI voice agents or chatbots you can’t build yet
  • You’re happy to stay the client-facing owner of every relationship

Not a fit if…

  • You want us to sell for you. We deliver; new business stays yours
  • You need someone on-site every day
  • The cheapest hourly rate matters more than quality
  • You expect us to take over or bill your clients directly
  • The scope changes daily and nobody on your side owns the brief
