Why it matters

Minutes decide who wins the lead

The scorecard weights the factors that most affect whether a lead becomes a customer.

First response speed

Leads often contact several businesses. The first useful reply usually sets the terms of the conversation.

Coverage

Evenings and weekends are when many people search and call. Voicemail is where many of those leads end.

Persistence

Most leads need several touches across channels before they reply. Many businesses stop after one.
