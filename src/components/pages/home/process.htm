How it runs

Four weeks from call to live system

No six-month discovery phase. We scope tightly, build in the open, and put something working in front of your customers fast.

  1. 01

    Week 1

    Scope call

    A 30-minute call to find the one workflow costing you the most. You leave with a fixed scope, a price and a date, whether or not you hire us.

  2. 02

    Week 2

    Build & train

    We ingest your docs, pricing and policies, wire the integrations, and set the guardrails for what the agent may decide on its own.

  3. 03

    Week 3

    Supervised pilot

    It goes live on a slice of real traffic with a human reviewing transcripts daily. We tune tone, edge cases and escalation rules against real conversations.

  4. 04

    Week 4+

    Handover & tune

    Full rollout, documentation, and a dashboard your team owns. We stay on for monthly tuning, or hand over the keys entirely. Your call.

Every engagement includes

  • Fixed-scope proposal before any work starts
  • Weekly demo you can actually click
  • Full source code and prompt ownership
  • Documented escalation and fallback paths
  • Team training session at handover
  • 30 days of post-launch tuning
