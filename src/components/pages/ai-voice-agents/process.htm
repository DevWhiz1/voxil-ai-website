Our Roadmap

How we build your AI Voice Agent

A structured, rapid-prototype process focused on conversational realism, low latency, and robust enterprise integrations.

01

Persona Design

We clone your ideal agent's voice, select custom pronunciations, and tune speech speed, gender, and regional accents.

02

Prompting & RAG

We script guardrails, set tone guidelines, and ingest knowledge base documents to ground the agent's real-time answers.

03

Telephony Setup

We provision live business lines, hook up Twilio or Telnyx SIP routing, and configure fallback numbers for human escalation.

04

Latency Tuning

We optimize server pathways and LLM response tokens to hit our sub-100ms lag threshold for realistic spoken dialogue.

05

API Sync & Launch

We link outbound scheduler APIs, CRM data syncs, and trigger systems, pushing the agent live to handle calls.
