Real Estate Case Study

Custom AI Integration for Automated Real Estate Lead Nurturing

How Voxil AI built an instant NLP lead qualification and scoring workflow, boosting conversion rates by 3x and streamlining lead distribution.

Client Vance & Co. Realty
Industry Real Estate Brokerage
Integrations HubSpot, Zapier, Twilio
Key Impact 3x Conversion Rate

Before Voxil AI

Vance & Co. Realty, a high-volume residential brokerage, was spending over $15,000 per month on Facebook Lead Ads and Google Ads. While this generated thousands of leads, agents struggled to follow up quickly. Fresh leads sat untouched for hours, often going cold. Agents spent 70% of their working hours making repetitive cold calls to unqualified leads, leading to low conversion rates, agent burnout, and wasted ad spend.

The Challenge

The brokerage needed a solution to capture, engage, qualify, and score leads in real-time, 24/7. The system had to instantly start conversations with new contacts via SMS, qualify them based on their budget, timeline, location preferences, and mortgage pre-approval status, and route high-intent leads to agents immediately.

The Solution

Voxil AI engineered an intelligent Real Estate Lead Engagement Workflow. Leveraging Natural Language Processing (NLP) over SMS and web interfaces, the system responds instantly to lead submissions, qualifiers them, and updates the CRM.

  • Instant Speed-to-Lead: Automated multi-channel outreach triggers within 90 seconds of lead submission.
  • AI Lead Qualification: Uses conversation trees to determine budget, purchasing timeline, and interest levels.
  • HubSpot Synchronization: Automatically updates lead profiles, logs chat transcripts, and schedules agent calls.

Key Systems Integrated & Replaced

Systems Replaced

  • • Manual lead assignment worksheets and delay-prone routing
  • • Generic, low-converting automated email follow-up templates
  • • Inefficient manual lead qualification calling protocols

Systems Integrated

  • • HubSpot CRM (deals, pipeline management, routing)
  • • Facebook Lead Ads & Google Ads Webhooks
  • • Twilio Programmable SMS API
  • • Zapier automation scripts

Strategic Takeaway

Automating the qualification step at the top of the funnel is the most direct way to increase ad ROI and agent efficiency. By responding to hot leads instantly via SMS, brokerages capture intent when it is at its highest point, ensuring that human efforts are reserved only for high-value client interactions.
