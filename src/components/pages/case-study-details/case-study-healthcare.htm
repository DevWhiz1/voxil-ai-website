Healthcare Case Study

AI Patient Triage Agent Development for Clinical Efficiency

How Voxil AI built CareFlow AI—a HIPAA-compliant triage assistant—reducing clinic wait times by 60% and cutting hours of manual administrative burden.

Client CareFlow Clinics
Industry Healthcare & Biotech
Integrations Epic Systems, Twilio
Key Impact 60% Wait Reduction

Before Voxil AI

CareFlow Clinics, a busy multi-location health system, was struggling under a massive backlog of patient calls and appointment booking requests. Patient intake was handled entirely by a receptionist team, leading to average call hold times of over 15 minutes. Routine queries regarding clinic hours, directions, and basic medical instructions frequently tied up the phone lines, making it difficult for patients with urgent issues to reach care coordinators.

The Challenge

The clinic network needed a solution that could capture and qualify patient concerns 24/7 without risking data leaks (maintaining absolute HIPAA compliance) or sacrificing clinical protocols. The new system had to communicate with patients empathetically, collect clinical symptoms accurately to prioritize urgent cases, and write that data directly into Epic Systems EHR to schedule appointments without human intervention.

The Solution

Voxil AI designed and deployed a HIPAA-compliant custom AI Agent called CareFlow Triage. Operating over SMS and webchat, this intelligent assistant guides patients through clinical intake protocol, checks real-time calendar availability, maps scheduling criteria, and writes directly to the EHR API.

  • Intelligent Intake: Empathetic conversation that securely logs patient symptoms and history.
  • Dynamic Patient Triage: Automatically identifies high-priority cases and escalates them to clinical staff immediately.
  • EHR Integration: Seamlessly writes patient details and booked slots directly into Epic Systems EHR.

Key Systems Integrated & Replaced

Systems Replaced

  • • Traditional legacy Interactive Voice Response (IVR) phone tree
  • • Manual paper intake forms and offline spreadsheets
  • • Outdated administrative scheduling workflows

Systems Integrated

  • • Epic Systems EHR (Electronic Health Record API)
  • • Twilio Communications Platform (SMS, voice channels)
  • • SendGrid API (patient confirmations & intake summary emails)

Strategic Takeaway

AI-driven patient triage demonstrates that routine administrative interactions in healthcare can be successfully automated without losing care quality. By integrating clinical routing logics directly with Epic Systems EHR at the messaging level, hospitals can eliminate bottlenecks, reduce cost-per-interaction, and provide immediate, reliable assistance.
